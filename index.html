About Us

A university-based service club part of the global Rotaract movement.

Service Above Self

The Rotaract Club of UoN Afya is affiliated with the University of Nairobi's Faculty of Health Sciences and forms part of the global Rotaract movement — an international network of young leaders dedicated to creating positive change through community service, leadership development, professional growth, and international understanding.

Beyond service, the club promotes fellowship and personal connections among members. Social activities including adventures, game nights, outings, and recreational events create opportunities for members to build friendships, strengthen teamwork, and enjoy a vibrant club experience outside academic settings.

The club also places significant emphasis on professional development through seminars, conferences, mentorship programmes, panel discussions, and professional talks — preparing members for leadership roles in their careers and communities.

  • Fellowship: Building lifelong friendships through socials and outings.
  • Leadership: Developing future leaders through mentorship and professional talks.
  • Service: Making meaningful contributions through community projects.

How to Join Us

Membership is open to individuals interested in service, leadership, and personal growth.

Membership Requirements

  • 1
    Declare your interest in joining the Rotaract Club of UoN Afya.
  • 2
    Attend at least three Rotaract meetings held on designated Thursdays at the University of Nairobi KNH Campus.
  • 3
    Participate in at least one club service project (giving back to the community) or one club social activity (e.g. game nights, adventures, outings).
  • 4
    Attend at least one meeting of our Mother Club — the Rotary Club of Madaraka, which meets at the Royal Nairobi Golf Club.
